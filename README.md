# WisdomRoots — Foundational Roots of the Wisdom Ecosystem

**Document class:** Foundational architecture and knowledge domains  
**Version:** 1.1-draft  
**Status:** Baseline definition — conceptual and documentary; not a claim of completed implementation  
**Parent authority:** WisdomCore (Layer 0)  
**Normative framework:** Eternal Wisdom Charter (WisdomPact)  
**Language policy:** Persian is primary; English is maintained as the interoperable secondary language.

## 1. Purpose

WisdomRoots defines the five foundational domains from which the Wisdom Ecosystem's concepts, knowledge, values, purposes, and methods of action are derived and critically examined.

The five roots form one coordinated foundational framework, while each root has its own dedicated repository for domain-specific documentation and development. The WisdomRoots repository is the shared index and integration point; it does not replace the five root repositories.

Roots are not operational product layers, business units, applications, or a substitute for the Charter. They provide foundations used by WisdomCore and consumed by the ecosystem's branches and systems.

## 2. The five foundational roots

| Root | Domain | Core question | Dedicated repository | Principal outputs |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Ontology — هستی‌شناسی | What exists, and what kinds of entities and relations are being described? | [WisdomOntology](https://github.com/Wisdom-Intercessor/WisdomOntology) | Controlled concepts, entity definitions, taxonomies, relation models and scope boundaries |
| 2 | Epistemology — معرفت‌شناسی | What can be known, by what methods, and with what evidence and uncertainty? | [WisdomEpistemology](https://github.com/Wisdom-Intercessor/WisdomEpistemology) | Claim records, evidence standards, provenance, uncertainty, validation and revision methods |
| 3 | Axiology — ارزش‌شناسی | Which values, rights, duties and impacts matter, and how are conflicts handled? | [WisdomAxiology](https://github.com/Wisdom-Intercessor/WisdomAxiology) | Ethical principles, rights safeguards, impact frameworks and explicit value-conflict analysis |
| 4 | Teleology — غایت‌شناسی | What purposes and ends are being pursued, and why? | [WisdomTeleology](https://github.com/Wisdom-Intercessor/WisdomTeleology) | Mission, vision, goals, outcome definitions and purpose traceability |
| 5 | Praxeology — کنش‌شناسی | How should deliberation become authorized, accountable and reviewable action? | [WisdomPraxeology](https://github.com/Wisdom-Intercessor/WisdomPraxeology) | Decision methods, action protocols, responsibility, feedback and learning cycles |

The five roots are complementary domains, not a ranking of human worth, belief systems, or disciplines. Their outputs remain open to criticism, evidence, pluralism, and revision under the Charter.

## 3. Relationship to the Wisdom Tree

`WisdomRoots (five coordinated root repositories) → WisdomCore (L0) → WisdomEconomy (Trunk) → WisdomBranches (D01–D08) → WisdomSystems (L1–L8)`

This sequence expresses conceptual and governance relationships, not physical nesting of Git repositories.

- **WisdomRoots:** shared index and integration framework for the five foundational domains.
- **Five root repositories:** maintain domain-specific definitions, research, evidence methods, values, purposes, and action methods.
- **WisdomCore (L0):** integrates foundations into shared architecture, standards, registry, and controlled change.
- **WisdomEconomy (Trunk):** connects roots, core, and branches; coordinates cross-domain economic architecture and horizontal economic capabilities.
- **WisdomBranches:** organizes civilizational macro-domains. Its D01–D08 taxonomy and mappings require controlled validation.
- **WisdomSystems (L1–L8):** eight operational system families with distinct domain ownership and interfaces.

This tree is an architectural and governance model. It does not imply that GitHub repositories are physically nested or that every conceptual domain requires a separate deployed service.

## 4. Cross-realm applicability

The roots inform work across all three realms:

- **Wis:** the human being, agency, cognition, conscience, rights, and personal responsibility.
- **Dom:** relationships, communities, institutions, knowledge exchange, economy, governance, and technology.
- **Land:** Earth, nature, living systems, resources, time, space, and the wider cosmos.

The same root may yield different methods and evidence requirements in each realm. Realm-specific application must be explicit; a general principle must not be presented as proof of a particular empirical claim.

## 5. Governance and quality rules

1. The Eternal Wisdom Charter is the normative framework; this repository does not supersede or amend it.
2. WisdomCore maintains Layer 0 architecture, cross-ecosystem coherence, and registry control within its documented remit.
3. Every foundational claim should distinguish definition, interpretation, empirical assertion, hypothesis, value judgment, and proposal.
4. Empirical claims require traceable sources, methods, uncertainty, and revision conditions.
5. Ethical and rights-based constraints must not be collapsed into a single numerical score.
6. Disagreement, minority positions, unresolved questions, and counter-evidence must be preserved where material.
7. Root definitions and mappings change through documented review and version control.
8. No root domain may claim exclusive authority over all other disciplines or over the Charter's open process of critique and revision.

## 6. Minimum document record

Each substantial root document should identify:
- document ID, title, version, language, and status;
- root domain and relevant realm(s);
- purpose, scope, definitions, and non-goals;
- claims and their type (conceptual, empirical, normative, or proposed);
- sources, evidence, provenance, and uncertainty;
- dependencies and affected architecture records;
- review history, unresolved issues, and approval state.

## 7. Current maturity and exclusions

This repository establishes the shared baseline and navigation for the five roots. The existence of the five dedicated repositories does not establish that all root specifications, datasets, research methods, or validation pipelines are complete.

This document does not authorize production services, financial instruments, identity services, governance mechanisms, or deployments.
